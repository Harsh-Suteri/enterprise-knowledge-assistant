# Acme Technologies — Employee Handbook (Extract)

## 1. Expense Reimbursement

Employees may claim reimbursement for business expenses incurred in the course of their duties.
Claims must be submitted through the internal portal within **30 days** of the expense being incurred.
Claims submitted after 30 days require written justification and are approved at the discretion of the Finance Head.

Expense claims up to ₹50,000 are approved by the employee's reporting manager.
Expense claims above ₹50,000 must be approved by both the reporting manager and the Finance Head.
Claims above ₹2,00,000 additionally require sign-off from the Chief Financial Officer.

Receipts are mandatory for every claim above ₹500. Claims without receipts will be rejected.

## 2. Refund and Cancellation Policy (Customer-Facing)

Customers may request a full refund within **14 days** of purchase, provided the service has not been
activated. Once a service is activated, refunds are prorated based on unused days remaining in the
billing cycle.

Refund requests are processed within 7 working days of approval. Refunds are issued to the original
payment method only. Requests made after 90 days from the date of purchase are not eligible for refund
under any circumstance.

## 3. Leave Policy

### 3.1 Annual Leave
Full-time employees accrue 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accruing at 1.5 days per month.
Up to 10 unused days may be carried forward into the following year. Any balance beyond 10 days lapses
on 31 March.

### 3.2 Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for any
absence exceeding 3 consecutive working days.

### 3.3 Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to **26 weeks** of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers are entitled
to **4 weeks** of paid parental leave, which must be taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption.
Parental leave is available to employees who have completed at least 80 days of service in the preceding
12 months.

## 4. Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with manager approval. Fully remote arrangements
require approval from the department head and are reviewed every 6 months. Employees working remotely
must be reachable during core hours of 11:00 to 17:00 IST.

## 5. Notice Period

The standard notice period is 30 days for employees below manager grade and 60 days for manager grade
and above. Notice period may be waived or shortened only with written approval from the reporting
manager and Human Resources.

## 6. Information Security

Company data must not be stored on personal devices or personal cloud accounts. All source code remains
the intellectual property of Acme Technologies. Sharing internal documents with third parties requires
a signed non-disclosure agreement.
