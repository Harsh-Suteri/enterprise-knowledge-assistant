# Brightline Health — System Access and Device Policy (Extract)

## 1. Account Provisioning

Access requests are raised by the hiring manager and fulfilled by the service desk within
**3 working days**. Accounts for contractors are created with a fixed expiry date not exceeding
**6 months**, which must be renewed explicitly.

Shared or generic accounts are prohibited except for approved service accounts, which are owned by a
named engineer and reviewed quarterly.

## 2. Authentication

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all remote access. Passwords must be at least
**14 characters** and are rotated every **180 days**. Reuse of the previous 10 passwords is blocked.

An account is locked after **5 consecutive failed sign-in attempts** and can be unlocked only by the
service desk after identity verification.

## 3. Privileged Access

Administrator rights are granted for a maximum of **8 hours** per request through the just-in-time
access tool. Standing administrator rights require an exception approved by the Chief Information
Security Officer and are reviewed every **30 days**.

All privileged sessions are recorded and retained for **1 year**.

## 4. Devices and Media

Only company-issued laptops may connect to the production network. Removable media is blocked by
default; exceptions require written approval from the Information Security team and are granted for a
maximum of **7 days**.

Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the service desk within **4 hours** of discovery.

## 5. Offboarding

All access is revoked on the employee's final working day. Mailbox contents are retained for
**90 days** after departure and then permanently deleted unless placed under legal hold.

Access reviews covering every production system are performed **twice per year** by system owners.
