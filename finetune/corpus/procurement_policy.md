# Calder Manufacturing — Procurement and Vendor Policy (Extract)

## 1. Purchase Authority

Purchase requisitions up to **25,000 rupees** may be approved by a team lead. Requisitions between
25,000 and 5,00,000 rupees require approval from the Category Manager. Any requisition above
**5,00,000 rupees** must be approved by the Procurement Director and recorded in the capital register.

Splitting a single purchase into smaller requisitions to stay below an approval threshold is
prohibited and is treated as a disciplinary matter.

## 2. Competitive Bidding

Purchases above 2,00,000 rupees require a minimum of **three written quotations**. Where fewer than
three vendors are capable of supplying the item, a single-source justification must be filed and
countersigned by the Procurement Director.

Tenders remain open for a minimum of **15 calendar days** from the date of publication.

## 3. Vendor Onboarding

New vendors must complete the onboarding pack, which includes tax registration, bank verification and
a signed code of conduct. Onboarding is completed within **12 working days** of receiving a complete
pack.

Vendors are re-assessed every **24 months**. A vendor scoring below 60 on the performance scorecard is
placed on a 90-day improvement plan before any termination decision is taken.

## 4. Payment Terms

Standard payment terms are **45 days** from the date of a valid invoice. Micro and small enterprises
registered under the applicable statute are paid within **15 days**.

Advance payment is capped at 20 percent of contract value and requires a bank guarantee of equal value.

## 5. Conflict of Interest

Employees must declare any financial interest in a vendor exceeding **2 percent** of that vendor's
share capital. Declarations are made to the Ethics Committee before participating in any evaluation.

Gifts from vendors valued above **2,000 rupees** must be declined or surrendered to the Ethics
Committee within 5 working days.
