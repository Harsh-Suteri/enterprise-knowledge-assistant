# Meridian Freight — Business Travel Policy (Extract)

## 1. Booking and Approval

All business travel must be booked through the Meridian travel desk at least **10 working days**
before departure. Bookings made inside 10 working days require approval from the Regional Director.

Domestic economy fares are approved by the line manager. International travel of any class requires
approval from both the line manager and the Head of Operations. Business class is permitted only for
flights with a scheduled duration exceeding **8 hours**.

Travel for groups of more than 6 employees to the same destination must be split across at least two
separate flights.

## 2. Daily Allowance

Employees travelling domestically receive a daily allowance of **1,800 rupees** covering meals and
incidentals. The international daily allowance is **65 US dollars**. Allowances are paid regardless of
whether receipts are produced, but are reduced by 40 percent on days where meals are provided by the
host or included in the conference fee.

Daily allowance is not payable for travel lasting less than 6 hours.

## 3. Accommodation

Hotel spend is capped at **6,500 rupees per night** in metro cities and 4,000 rupees per night
elsewhere. Stays exceeding 14 consecutive nights must be converted to serviced apartments, which are
booked directly by the facilities team.

Employees may retain hotel loyalty points earned on business travel for personal use.

## 4. Expense Submission

Travel expense claims must be filed within **21 days** of return. Claims filed later than 21 days are
settled in the following quarter and require a written explanation to Finance.

Foreign currency expenses are converted at the rate published by the company on the first day of the
travel month, not the rate on the transaction date.

## 5. Travel Insurance and Safety

All international travellers are automatically enrolled in the corporate travel insurance scheme.
Travel to countries rated Tier 3 or above on the internal risk register requires written clearance
from the Security Office and is reviewed every 30 days.

Employees must register their itinerary in the traveller tracking system before departure.
